  WHAT WAS MISSING IN OPTION 1
    It was a list wearing a map costume: a line, circles and labels on a flat white sheet. The contours meant nothing,
    nothing had weight or depth, nothing moved, and pressing a station could not take you anywhere because there was no "there".

  THE IDEA: A HILL YOU CAN FLY OVER
    1. The ground means something. The sheet is a hill of flat terraces (five flat tones, no gradients), and altitude is the
       match with the job: the trail steps onto a new terrace exactly where the match rises (30%, 50%, 70% where you stand,
       90% after the next station, the white cap at 100%). Every terrace has a thin edge on the side that faces you.
    2. The trail is one road, not a thread: a white bed from «البداية» to the flag, filled solid navy as far as you have
       climbed, then the accent dots of your next leg, then grey dots to the summit. Start, here, next, summit read in one look.
    3. Things stand on the sheet. Coins have a 3 px edge and a ground shadow, the flag has a pole, the walker has a tag.
       They are billboards: when the camera pitches they stay upright, so the map is a place and not a picture.
    4. One camera. The opening levels the sheet toward you; pressing a station pitches it back and flies to that station.

  THE LINE ("fully straight, a little move"): both readings are served.
    The course is one straight climb, bottom to top, stations in order. The line itself has a gentle natural sway
    (x = cx + two slow sines, about ±24 px on desktop, ±13 px on the phone). And it lives: the dots of the way ahead (and of
    your next leg) drift slowly toward the summit, one dot spacing every 1.9 s. Nothing else moves at rest except one slow
    pulse on the current station.

  CONTENT: the demo right after the sample CV (spec 10.4 step 7): خالد · level 2 مستكشف · 180 / 350 XP · rank 18 · match 70%.
    Stations 1-6 done (proved by the CV), 7 Power BI ★ is the next step (10 h · 5 lessons · +200 XP · 70% → 90%), 8 الإحصاء ★★ later,
    then the summit «جاهز للوظيفة». Branches: أخصائي تقارير وبيانات 50% → 80%, محلل ذكاء الأعمال 43% → 60%.
    Layout takes 4 to 8 stations (node final-home.build.mjs --stations 4 writes final-home-4.html, which also shows a locked station).

  STRUCTURE (for the React port)
    .map (window, clips) > .cam (perspective 1100 px; phone 820 px) > .plane (preserve-3d: terrain svg + trail svg)
    > .bb billboards (a.st[data-station][data-x][data-y][data-r], .flag, .you, .peak). The card lives outside the camera in
    .flayer (desktop: a card on the map; phone: a bottom sheet above the tab bar). All opening times are the object O in the
    build script; all flight times are the object F at the top of the script below.
