01 · Brand   the mark, the wordmark «مساري», lockups, small sizes, app tile

Mark on stage
Mark on vellum

An astrolabe reduced to three facts.

A dial that is almost closed, a path of three stations that grow as they near the opening, and the star the dial is waiting for. The ring stops short of the star, the way lines on a star chart never touch the star they lead to: that gap is the signature carried into every icon, avatar and screen. Read as a progress ring it says what the product says: you are most of the way there and the next step is already plotted.

64 grid · ring r 24, weight 6.4 · opening 60° on the 135° axis · star r 6 · stations r 2.2 / 3.5 / 5, all on that axis. Two optical cuts: an engraved one with its tick limb for large sizes, a heavier one without station dots for 32 px and below.

سلسلة 3 أيام

What moves. Nothing slides in a straight line. The tick limb turns very slowly, the way a sky turns (one revolution in four minutes). Every six seconds one story plays, piece by piece, then rests: the brass line is engraved from the finished station to the current one; the +200 XP token leaves the dial and travels along its dotted arc to the XP bar; the bar fills and its end point moves; Sara's row rises one place past the row above it; the scan line crosses the CV and the third skill chip is plotted in. The current station's bezel ticks once a second. This frame is caught at 1.5 s: the token is mid-arc, the rows are mid-swap, the line is half engraved.
