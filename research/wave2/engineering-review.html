مهندس موقع (مدني) Site / Civil Engineer

يكون في الموقع كل يوم: يتأكد إن البناء ماشي حسب المخططات والمواصفات، وينسّق مع المقاولين، ويحل مشاكل التنفيذ قبل ما تأخّر المشروع.

المسار: المخططات الهندسية و AutoCAD ← تنفيذ الأعمال والإشراف على الموقع ← كود البناء السعودي (SBC) ← ضبط الجودة وضمانها (QA/QC) ← الصحة والسلامة المهنية (HSE) ← communication

المسار مبني على: مواد اختبار المنشئ المشارك CAC Level I من AIC، ودرجات الاعتماد المهني في دليل الهيئة السعودية للمهندسين

المراجع الإرشادية:

  • Certified Associate Constructor (CAC) Level I: exam content areas
    • 1 Communication Skills
    • 2 Engineering Management
    • 3 Management Concepts
    • 4 Materials, Methods & Project Modeling
    • 5 Bidding and Estimating
    • 6 Budgeting, Costs & Cost Control
    • 7 Planning, Scheduling & Schedule Control
    • 8 Construction Safety
    • 9 Construction Geomatics
    • 10 Project Administration
  • Professional Accreditation Guide for Engineering Categories, Version (1) 1446 AH / 2024 AD: professional grades
    • 1 Engineer/Architect Grade: «Hold a Bachelor's degree from an accredited academic program in one of the engineering/architectural specializations…»
    • 2 Associate Grade: «Be a registered member of the Authority» · «Pass the Fundamentals of Engineering/Architecture exam (FE/FA)» · «Pay classification fees»
    • 3 Professional Grade: «Have a minimum experience of five years in a similar role proved» · «Pass the Professional Engineering/Architecture Practice Exam (PE/PA)»
    • 4 Consultant Grade: «practicing at the professional level for at least five years, or a minimum of ten years of professional practice»

AIC CAC Level I lists «Materials, Methods & Project Modeling» before «Construction Safety» and «Project Administration»; reading drawings comes first because every posting checked asks it before site supervision (Teef Najd: «Ability to read and interpret engineering drawings.»). The Saudi Building Code is placed after drawings (Dalil's call: it is applied to what is drawn and built; no guideline orders it). QA/QC and HSE sit beside the trunk, not after it: both postings ask them at ★1 alongside site work.
