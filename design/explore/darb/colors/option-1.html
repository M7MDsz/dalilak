the idea

One trail, folded into two contour arches, with a waypoint between its feet. The hill and the way up it are the same line: you do not look at the mountain, you walk it.

It reads as a summit seen through its contours, as a gate you step through, and as the opening letter of a path. The coral dot is «أنت هنا»: the same waypoint sits on every icon and at the end of every progress line in the product.

construction · 24 grid
One family, three rules.
  1. One weight: 1.75 px stroke on a 24 px grid, 2 px margin, round caps and joins everywhere.
  2. One corner: radius 2 on boxes, true arcs on rings; nothing is squared off, nothing is filled.
  3. One waypoint: every icon carries exactly one coral dot (r 1.75), placed where the trail stops: the end of an open stroke, the summit, the keyhole, the gap in a ring. Outlines stay open wherever the object allows it, so the line reads as a trail and the dot as the place you are heading.

What moves. The night panel is one continuous shot of the journey, on a 14 s loop. The contour lines drift very slowly (a 40 s parallax of a few pixels). The trail DRAWS itself from the bottom edge upward (stroke-dashoffset, 1.6 s per leg): dotted first, then solid ink behind a coral drawing head. As the head reaches each waypoint the waypoint settles (scale .6 to 1, one 1 px contour ripple) and its product piece slides in 18 px along its leader line while fading in (420 ms): 1 the CV sheet, where a scan line travels down, phrases get highlighted right to left and skill chips pop one by one; 2 the job card, whose match ring is drawn to 70% while the figure counts up; 3 the XP bar, where "+200 XP" rises from the waypoint and lands on the bar, the segment fills and the number rolls; 4 the leaderboard pill, where the rank rolls from 7 to 6. Last, the flag is raised on the summit and Daleel's bubble pops out of the corner. The frame above is caught at about 8 s: the head is between waypoints 3 and 4, the XP chip is mid-flight (its two echoes are the motion trail), the second skill chip and the rank pill are still arriving. Nothing glows, nothing bounces; reduced-motion shows the finished map.
