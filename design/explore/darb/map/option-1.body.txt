    covered trail      a solid ink line from the trailhead to «أنت هنا»
    you                the vermilion waypoint where the solid line ends, with Sara's tag (avatar · أنت هنا · 70%)

    the way ahead      ink dots through hollow dashed markers 4, 5, 6 to the summit flag
    after the summit   two thin dashed side trails to two small peaks (the branch jobs)
    labels             always on the same side of the marker, at the same distance, on the same short leader

  WHAT STAYS DARB
