التوصية: Jooble REST API. It is the only one of the three whose public docs state a free limit (100 requests a day per key, reset at midnight UTC) and a simple request (POST JSON with keywords and location). It has a Saudi site (sa.jooble.org) and each country gets its own key, so ask for the Saudi key. Each result carries the original link, source, company and update date, which fits our cards. Its API page says results may be shown on your site in your own design. Careerjet's docs do not confirm en_SA and need the user's IP and browser on every call. JSearch's free quota and Saudi coverage could not be confirmed from pages that open without sign-up, and it resurfaces LinkedIn, Indeed and Glassdoor postings, the sites we chose not to scrape.
